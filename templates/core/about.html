Ku Saabsan Nidaamka Maareynta Maktabadda

Nidaamkan waxaa loo sameeyay si loo beddelo habka wax-u-qaadista maktabadda ee gacanta laga qabto oo loo rogo hab dijital ah oo casri ah. Wuxuu u fududeeyaa daabacaadda buugaagta, diiwaangelinta xubnaha, amaahda iyo soo celinta buugaagta, xisaabinta ganaaxyada, iyo soo saarista warbixino sax ah.

Hadafka Guud

In la naqshadeeyo oo la horumariyo Nidaam Maareynta Maktabadda oo otomaatig ka dhiga hawlaha maktabadda, hagaajiya waxqabadka, oo horumariya maamulka kheyraadka maktabadda labadaba shaqaalaha iyo xubnaha.

Waxa Nidaamku Bixiyo

  • Diiwaangelinta iyo maamulka buugaagta (ku dar, wax ka beddel, tirtir, raadi)
  • Diiwaangelinta xubnaha maktabadda
  • Amaahda iyo soo celinta buugaagta
  • Xisaabinta ganaaxyada dib u dhaca
  • Dalbashada buugaagta la qaatay (Reservation)
  • Warbixino faah faahsan oo ku saabsan buugaagta, xubnaha iyo amaahda
